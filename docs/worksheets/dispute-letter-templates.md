# Dispute Letter Templates (copy-paste)

These are the full templates behind [Chapter 4](../04-dispute-letters.md).
Replace every `[bracket]` and delete any line that doesn't apply. Keep letters
under one page. Send copies of bills/EOBs, never the originals, and note the
send date in your [claims log](../07-claims-log.md).

## T1 — General review request

```
[Your name]
[Your address]
[Your phone / email]
[Date]

Billing Department
[Provider name and address]

RE: Claim [number], account [number], date of service [date]

Dear Billing Department,

I'm writing to ask for a review of the bill for my [date] visit with
[provider/clinic name]. My Explanation of Benefits shows my share as [$X],
but the bill I received asks for [$Y]. The two documents don't agree.

Could you compare this claim against my insurer's explanation and send me a
corrected statement if the numbers should change? Copies of both documents
are enclosed.

Could you reply within 30 days? I'd like to keep the account clear before
any due dates pass.

Thank you for your help — I know billing is a busy department.

Sincerely,
[Your signature]
[Your name]
Enclosures: copy of bill, copy of EOB (please do not return)
```

## T2 — Duplicate charge

```
[Header block as in T1]

RE: Duplicate charge — claim [number], date of service [date]

Dear Billing Department,

The enclosed bill lists the same charge more than once: [service or
description] appears [twice] on [dates/lines]. My EOB for [date of service]
shows this service once, with my share of [$X].

Could you remove the duplicate line(s) and send a corrected statement?

Could you also confirm the corrected total before any due date, so the
account isn't sent onward in error? I'd appreciate a reply within 30 days.

Sincerely,
[Signature]
Enclosures: copy of bill, copy of EOB
```

## T3 — Service you did not receive

```
[Header block as in T1]

RE: Claim [number], date of service [date] — service in question

Dear Billing Department,

The enclosed bill includes [service/description] on [claimed date]. To my
knowledge I did not receive that service on that date; my visit was on
[different date] and involved [what actually happened, briefly].

Could you review the record and the claim, remove any service not
performed, and send a corrected statement?

Copies of the bill and my EOB are enclosed. I'd appreciate a reply within
30 days, and before any due date on the account.

Sincerely,
[Signature]
Enclosures: copy of bill, copy of EOB
```

## T4 — Out-of-network / surprise charge

```
[Header block as in T1]

RE: Claim [number], date of service [date] — network status question

Dear Billing Department,

My visit on [date] took place at [facility], which is in my network. The
enclosed bill includes a charge from [person/lab/service] that I did not
choose and did not see separately during the visit.

Could you tell me whether this item was in or out of network? If it was out
of network, could you confirm whether pricing protection applies for this
kind of visit, and reprocess or correct the charge if so?

Copies of the bill and EOB are enclosed. I'd appreciate a reply within 30
days, and please hold any collection activity while this is reviewed.

Sincerely,
[Signature]
Enclosures: copy of bill, copy of EOB
```

## T5 — Financial-assistance request

```
[Header block as in T1]

RE: Account [number] — request for financial assistance review

Dear Billing Department,

I'm writing to ask whether this account qualifies for financial assistance
or a charity-care discount under your organization's policy. Paying the
amount owed would be a hardship for my household.

Could you send your financial-assistance application and tell me:
- what documents you need, and the deadline;
- whether the application pauses collection activity while it is reviewed;
- whether any prompt-pay or self-pay discount could apply in the meantime.

Thank you — I'd like to get the paperwork in well before the due date.

Sincerely,
[Signature]
Enclosures: none yet (awaiting application and document list)
```

## Mailing checklist (works for all five)

```
[ ] Fill every bracket, delete unused lines
[ ] Attach COPIES of bill + EOB (keep originals)
[ ] Send with tracking; write the date in the claims log
[ ] Calendar a follow-up for letter-date + 30 days
[ ] File the sent copy with the claim in your folder
```
