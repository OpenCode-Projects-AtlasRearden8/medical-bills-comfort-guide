# The Medical Bills Comfort Guide

## Start Here

Something arrived in the mail. It has numbers on it, and some of the numbers
are alarming. Take a breath. You are going to be fine — this is paperwork, not
an emergency, and it almost always has more give in it than it first appears.

This manual is a **plain-English guide to reading your own medical paperwork**:
bills, Explanation of Benefits letters (EOBs), deductibles, and all the rest.
It will not tell you anything about medical care, because that is not our lane
and never will be. It will help you get the *administrative* side of healthcare
under control, one page at a time.

**This is admin help only. Nothing here is medical advice, legal advice, or
financial advice.** We do not give medical advice. For anything about your
actual care, ask your doctor. For anything about your specific situation, your
insurer's member services line (the number on your insurance card) is the right
place to start, and this guide will show you how to use it well.

## Who this is for

Anyone who has ever looked at a medical bill and felt their stomach drop.
You do not need to be good at math, or paperwork, or phone calls. Every chapter
uses round numbers ($100 deductible, $50 visits) so the *idea* stays clear and
you can swap in your own real numbers later.

## How to use this guide

- New bill in hand? Start with [Bill vs. EOB, in Plain Words](01-bill-vs-eob.md).
- Confused by what you "owe"? Do the round-number math in
  [Deductible and Out-of-Pocket, with Big Easy Numbers](02-deductible-and-out-of-pocket.md).
- Something looks wrong? It might be — see
  [Common Billing Errors and How to Spot Them](03-common-billing-errors.md).
- Ready to say "please recheck this"? Grab a template from
  [Dispute Letters and How to Send Them](04-dispute-letters.md).
- Worried you can't afford it? Read [Financial Assistance: Questions to Ask](05-financial-assistance.md)
  and [Payment Plans Without Stress](06-payment-plans.md).
- Want to stop juggling this in your head? Set up
  [Your Own Claims Log](07-claims-log.md), and print the worksheets in
  [worksheets/](worksheets/claims-log-worksheet.md).
- Have to make a call? [08 Phone Scripts](08-phone-scripts.md) gives you
  word-for-word openings, holds, and exits.

## The short version

1. **The bill and the EOB are different documents.** Compare them before paying anything.
2. **Nothing urgent happens if you take two weeks to read paperwork.**
3. **Billing errors are common.** You are allowed to ask questions and ask for corrections.
4. **Financial assistance exists** at most hospitals, and asking never hurts your care.
5. **A payment plan is a normal, boring tool** — most providers expect to offer them.
6. **Write things down.** A simple claims log turns chaos into rows.
